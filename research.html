研究合作Research

欢迎高校、实验室与开源社区就具身宠物大脑、S2 Em-Situ 空间系统与 RSI 过程方法开展公开层合作。合作范围限于已脱敏的过程、数据集接口与展示端，底座模型与研究目标不进入对外材料。

Universities, labs, and open-source groups are welcome to collaborate on embodied pet brains, S2 Em-Situ, and RSI process methods. Collaboration stays at the public layer: process, interfaces, and demos. Core models and targets stay private.
