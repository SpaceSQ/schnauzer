雪纳瑞机器人
Schnauzer Robot

具身AI宠物大脑 Embodied AI Pet Brain

A S2 ROBOT, A RSI ROBOT

雪纳瑞机器人（Schnauzer Robot）是一款基于 S2 Em-Situ 环境系统研发的具身 AI 宠物大脑。该项目由广州雪纳瑞机器人有限公司 Miles Xiang 率领 SCR 团队进行研发。项目全部开源，每日更新进展，每周发布迭代版本。 Schnauzer Robot is an embodied AI pet brain built on the S2 Em-Situ environment system. Guangzhou Schnauzer Robot Co., Ltd., led by Miles Xiang and the SCR team, develops it in the open: daily progress updates and a weekly release.

The lab room is 4m × 9m × 2.4m, origin at the lower-left, in millimeters. Slot 1 is the human avatar, slot 2 the schnauzer, slot 3 the husky, slots 4–9 are visitors. Fine models are not on this page.
